## LinkedIn post — ready to copy

**When you haven’t looked for a job in years, where do you start?**

Being made redundant can suddenly put you back into a process that feels unfamiliar.

Which roles should you look for? How does your experience fit what employers want? Where should you focus your effort—and how do you keep track of it all?

Having experienced redundancy myself, I’m building **RoleBridge** to help IT jobseekers navigate their way towards their next role, one step at a time. This free beta initially focuses on release/change management, technology delivery and IT service roles.

It brings the main parts of a job search into one place:

- ✅ Understand your experience and explore possible job directions.
- ✅ Find vacancies and check how your experience connects to the requirements.
- ✅ Prepare for interviews with practical questions, sample answers and downloadable preparation packs.
- ✅ Track applications, responses and next steps so you can see your progress.

Whether you’re facing redundancy or simply looking for a change, the aim is to make the process feel more manageable—especially if it’s been a long time since you last had to do it.
