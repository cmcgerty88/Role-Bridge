How was your experience?

Seven short questions. About 2–3 minutes. Tell us what helped and what needs work—honest feedback is useful, even if you got stuck.

Only the answers you enter here are sent. Your CV, profile, saved jobs and interview notes are not attached. Please leave out names, CV text, employer details and other private or confidential information.

How your feedback is handled

Feedback is voluntary. We don’t ask for your name or email. Your answers are sent through Formspree to RoleBridge Admin’s private inbox to improve this beta. Formspree also processes request information such as IP addresses for service operation and spam prevention; this form is not a guarantee of anonymity.

RoleBridge Admin plans to delete feedback emails, provider submissions and any response exports 30 days after this pilot ends. This is a manual process. Browser data controls on the main site cannot delete feedback already sent. Because we don’t ask for identifying details, we may not be able to locate a particular response.

This page does not read or save your workspace. Unsent answers stay in this tab and can be cleared below. You can also use the written questions for a feedback-handling query, without including CV content.

Preview only: you can select answers and type below, but sending is not available yet. Your answers stay in this tab only and will be lost if you refresh or close it. Nothing is sent or saved automatically.
