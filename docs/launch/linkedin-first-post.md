Looking for your next IT role—and not sure where to start?

If you haven't looked for work in years, finding your next role can feel like a job in itself.

We're building RoleBridge to give IT jobseekers a clearer path from their experience to their next role, one step at a time. Our first beta focuses on release/change management, technology delivery and IT service roles.

Whether you're facing redundancy, returning to work or ready for a change, you can:

✅ Explore roles that connect with your experience.
✅ Find vacancies and check job requirements.
✅ Prepare for interviews with questions, sample answers and printable packs.
✅ Track applications, responses and your next steps.

RoleBridge is free during beta, and we're inviting IT jobseekers to help shape it.

Could you spare 10–15 minutes? Try one task that matters to your job search, then tell us what helped, what confused you and what you'd change.

👉 Try the website: https://cmcgerty88.github.io/Role-Bridge/

📝 Share your feedback: https://cmcgerty88.github.io/Role-Bridge/feedback/

No account is needed to try the website. Your CV is processed in your browser rather than uploaded to a server. You can use sample information if you prefer.

Know someone who is looking for work? Please pass this on. Their honest feedback could help make the next person's job search a little clearer.
