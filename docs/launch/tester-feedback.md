# RoleBridge — beta feedback questionnaire

Status: ready-to-import draft, not a live Microsoft Form. Organiser label supplied by the owner: **RoleBridge Admin**. The owner supplied the public contact address below. Confirm the participant notice and retention period before collecting responses.

## Form title

RoleBridge beta — tell us what you think

## Form description

Thanks for trying RoleBridge. These seven questions take about 2–3 minutes and will help us improve the job-search journey. Please answer based on what you actually tried; it's useful to hear where something didn't work.

Feedback is voluntary. We do not ask for your name or email. Please leave out CV text, names, employer details, contact information and other private or confidential information. You can skip the written questions.

Your responses are collected through Microsoft Forms and will be reviewed by RoleBridge Admin to improve the beta. The proposed retention period is 30 days after this pilot ends, after which responses and downloaded response files will be deleted. This is a planned manual process, not automatic deletion. Because we do not ask for identifying details, we may not be able to locate a particular response.

Questions about your feedback: **colinmcgerty88@gmail.com**.

## Questions and settings

### 1. What was the main thing you wanted RoleBridge to help you do today?

Type: Choice. One answer. Required.

- Work out which roles might suit my experience
- Find vacancies to explore
- Check my experience against a job description
- Prepare for an interview
- Track applications or decide what to do next
- Look around without a specific task
- Something else

Keep “Something else” as a plain choice; an extra free-text response is not necessary.

### 2. How easy or difficult was it to work out where to start?

Type: Choice. One answer. Required.

- Very easy
- Quite easy
- Neither easy nor difficult
- Quite difficult
- Very difficult
- I didn't get far enough to judge

### 3. Were you able to complete what you came to do?

Type: Choice. One answer. Required.

- Yes, without help
- Yes, with help from someone
- Partly
- No
- I was only looking around

### 4. How useful was the guidance or output you received for your job search?

Type: Choice. One answer. Required.

- Very useful
- Quite useful
- Neither useful nor unhelpful
- Not very useful
- Not at all useful
- I didn't receive or try any guidance or output

### 5. Where, if anywhere, did you get stuck or find something confusing, inaccurate or missing?

Type: Text. Long answer. Optional.

Subtitle: Name the page or feature and describe the issue briefly. Please don't paste CV text or personal details.

### 6. How likely are you to use RoleBridge again for a future job-search task?

Type: Choice. One answer. Required.

- Very likely
- Quite likely
- Neither likely nor unlikely
- Quite unlikely
- Very unlikely
- I'm not sure yet

### 7. What one change would make RoleBridge more useful to you?

Type: Text. Long answer. Optional.

Subtitle: A sentence or two is enough. Please don't include personal or confidential information.

## Completion message

Thank you for helping shape RoleBridge. Your feedback will help us prioritise improvements. You can return to the website at https://cmcgerty88.github.io/Role-Bridge/.

## How to use the feedback

Look at the task chosen in question 1 alongside task completion (3) and usefulness (4). Questions 2 and 5 show navigation and content problems; question 7 helps prioritise a next improvement. Treat question 6 as stated intention, not proof of repeat use or demand. Report counts with the sample size for a small pilot, rather than implying statistical certainty. Keep raw feedback and response exports out of this public repository.
