# RoleBridge beta: initial IT focus and next-version reminders

Owner decision, 26 October 2026: keep RoleBridge in beta for testing, initially focused on IT jobseekers in the UK and Northern Ireland. Prioritise the existing release/change management, technology delivery and IT service guidance. Other roles remain accessible; this positioning does not add specialist questions for every IT discipline or filter out existing saved jobs.

The promise to test is: **a clearer path to your next IT role, from understanding your experience and choosing jobs to interview preparation and tracking progress.** The beta remains free. Feedback question 8 tests stated willingness to pay at £4.99, £7.99, £9.99 per month or more, with explicit no-payment and unsure choices. This is research, not a pricing launch or payment authorisation.

## Remind the owner before planning the next version

- **Cloudflare: deferred, not connected.** Revisit a small backend for company-website research and private API credentials. Cloudflare provides infrastructure; connecting it alone does not improve interview answers. Any website retrieval needs public-destination validation, redirect controls, time/size limits, rate limiting, source attribution and a clear user action. Candidate CVs and interview notes should not be sent for a company-URL lookup. Current company research is pasted and reviewed manually.
- **OpenAI: deferred, not connected.** Revisit more tailored role-specific questions, answer coaching and research synthesis after tester feedback identifies the useful gaps. Before implementation, agree explicit consent, the minimum reviewed data to send, secret handling on the backend, provider retention/deletion terms, costs and usage limits. Evaluate sample packs for accuracy, usefulness and invented claims; better output is a hypothesis to test, not an automatic consequence of adding AI.
- **Pricing page: deferred.** Once tester interviews and actual usage show which parts people value, decide whether RoleBridge should offer a free tier, a paid candidate plan, or another model. Use the beta's willingness-to-pay answers as a starting signal, then test real purchase intent. Only publish prices, billing terms and feature limits when a paid offer and support process are ready; do not treat the survey's £4.99/£7.99/£9.99 options as agreed prices.
- **Employer outplacement page and package: deferred.** Explore a separate page for employers supporting people after redundancy, explaining the candidate journey, what the employer buys, onboarding, accessibility, support, reporting and a contact route. Validate the offer with a few employers and outplacement contacts before building employer accounts or contracts. Define clearly that a candidate's CV, job search, interview notes and individual outcomes stay private from the former employer unless the candidate explicitly chooses to share something. Agree procurement, data roles, retention and UK/EU privacy terms before launching a paid employer service.
- Keep the current browser-only candidate workflow and free beta until the owner chooses the next scope. The existing Formspree feedback delivery is separate. Do not introduce billing, hosted candidate accounts or new data sharing as part of this reminder.

These are reminders for future development sessions, not scheduled notifications or permission to connect services or launch pricing now.

## Where to find the first IT testers

1. Start with the founder's personal LinkedIn network, pointing to the RoleBridge page and a specific 10–15 minute test. Explain that it is free, in beta, and initially focused on IT roles.
2. Ask IT recruiters and outplacement contacts in Northern Ireland and the wider UK to share the invitation with people who may benefit. Ask for feedback rather than implying endorsement.
3. Seek permission to post in relevant IT service management, release/change management, delivery and local technology jobseeker groups. Follow group rules; do not mass-message members.
4. Use the company LinkedIn page for a clear invitation and updates. Defer paid advertising until the first cohort can complete a task and describe the value without help.

Review task completion, usefulness, confusing steps and pricing responses together. Record sample size and treat willingness-to-pay answers as intentions; they are not evidence of actual purchases. No posts, messages or adverts have been sent by the developer.
