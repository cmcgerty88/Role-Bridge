Your private workspace

Continue where you left off.

Create a workspace on this device. No email is needed. Your reviewed profile and job-search progress will be encrypted and saved here as you work.

This is device signup, with no online account or cross-device sync. Use a trusted personal browser. Your password cannot be reset: losing it means losing access. Saved data expires 30 days after the last save, and is removed when you next open the app. The workspace locks after 10 minutes without activity.

Use 14–256 characters, including at least one uppercase letter and one special character (for example !, @ or #). Don’t reuse a password from another service.

A shared or compromised device, browser extensions, or malicious site updates can still expose an unlocked workspace. Avoid full addresses, dates of birth, identity documents, health details and referees’ information.

Local library · no AI connection
Starting points, not a decision about your career.

These directions come from a limited library of business, service and technology-delivery roles. Your target role takes priority; otherwise we use your current role. They are not live vacancies or a qualification assessment.

How these directions are chosen

We put title connections first. Other suggestions must share a supported work area and responsibility level, with at least two confirmed skill connections including one linked to the role’s main work. Without a role title we use those skill rules alone. Titles never add skills or establish qualifications. If your occupation is outside the library, we say so instead of suggesting unrelated work. Browse the whole library if you deliberately want to explore a change of direction.

CV and interview downloads are unencrypted documents you choose to create for use outside the app. Downloads can be restored on this landing page using your password before their expiry.
